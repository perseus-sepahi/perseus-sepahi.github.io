## Requirements

A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.

## Making AI subtitles

1. Open a video with speech.
2. Click the sparkle button in the controls, or press **I**, then **Generate Subtitles**.
3. The first time, download the speech model when asked (Whisper Large v3 Turbo, 574 MB). Smaller models are in **Settings ▸ Speech to Text**.

The part you're watching is transcribed first. Subtitles are saved, so reopening the video brings them back instantly.

## Translating subtitles

Pick a language under **Translate** in the AI panel. Kinoglot needs one of these:

- **Ollama** (free and private): install it from [ollama.com](https://ollama.com). Kinoglot finds it and downloads the recommended translation model from **Settings ▸ Translation**.
- **Your own API key** for OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Google Gemini, entered in **Settings ▸ Translation**.

## Subtitle files next to a video

macOS only lets App Store apps read the files you open. To load subtitle files that sit next to a video, choose **Subtitles ▸ Find Subtitles Next to This Video** and click **Allow**. Kinoglot remembers the folder. You can also drag a subtitle file onto the window.

## Kinoglot Pro

- The player is free. AI subtitles and translation are free for your first 2 hours of watching with AI subtitles on screen.
- **Kinoglot Pro** is a one-time purchase that unlocks them for good. No subscription.
- Bought Pro on another Mac, or reinstalled? Choose **Restore Purchases** in the Kinoglot Pro window or in **Settings ▸ General**, signed in with the same Apple Account.
- Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## A file won't play

Kinoglot plays almost every format, but protected (DRM) videos, such as purchases from other stores, can't be played by any third-party player. If a normal file fails, please email me its format and I'll look into it.
